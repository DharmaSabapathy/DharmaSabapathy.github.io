Selected work

Projects

Design work from two internships and four seasons of competition robotics. Each write-up covers the same ground: what the thing had to do, the decision that mattered, and how it was actually built.

  • 2026 · NASA JSC

    Lunar Terrain Vehicle front trunk

    A self-opening hood for NASA’s LTV ground test unit. Calculated the centre of mass and the gas-spring force to lift it, then designed the sheet-metal structure, billet mounts and latch into a chassis I inherited and could not move.

    • SOLIDWORKS
    • Gas springs
    • Sheet metal
    • Packaging

  • 2025 · Apptronik

    Humanoid robot HMI handles

    Control handles for physically manipulating a humanoid robot, designed from scratch. Revised against beta-tester feedback on button placement and ergonomics; FDM to iterate, SLS to deliver, drawn for injection moulding at scale.

    • Onshape
    • Ergonomics
    • SLS
    • DFM

  • 2022–26 · FRC 2468

    FRC 2468 competition robot

    Whole-robot integration plus two subsystems designed from scratch. A master sketch fixed every interface and space claim before anyone detailed a part; the transfer moved from live axles to a dead-axled timing belt; the hopper traded a clever deployment for one that couldn’t jam. Competed at the FIRST Championship.

    • Master sketch
    • Integration
    • Mechanism design
    • Dead axle
    • Bearing stackups

  • 2025 · NASA SEES

    Quiet supersonic aircraft research

    Led aircraft configuration development on a national research team studying sonic-boom reduction for commercial-scale supersonic flight. CAD and CFD; presented to NASA engineers and at the AGU 2025 Annual Meeting.

    • CFD
    • Aerodynamics
    • Research
